❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Quant Terminal really free?
Yes, 100% free. No premium tier, no paywalled features, no hidden costs. We cover our expenses through non-intrusive advertising.

Do you store my trading data?
No. All strategy calculations run in your browser. We don't have access to your parameters, results, or any personal data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use this for actual trading?
Quant Terminal is a simulation and education tool. It does not connect to any exchange, execute trades, or manage real funds. Always do your own research before trading with real money.

Can I suggest a new strategy?
Absolutely. Use the form above with "Feature Request" selected and describe the strategy logic. We evaluate all community suggestions for future updates.
