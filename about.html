🎯 Our Mission

Crypto trading is dominated by emotion, hype, and guesswork. We believe the antidote is data. Quant Terminal exists to give every trader — regardless of experience level — access to the same kind of backtesting tools that professional quant funds use to validate their ideas.

Our goal is simple: help you lose less money. By testing strategies on historical data before committing real capital, you can separate what "feels right" from what actually works. That insight alone can save months of expensive trial-and-error.

⚙ What We Built

Quant Terminal is a browser-based backtesting engine. No downloads, no accounts, no API keys. You pick a coin, choose a strategy, adjust parameters, and instantly see how that strategy would have performed over the past year.

The platform includes three distinct strategies:

  • TRIPLE — Bollinger Bands + RSI + MACD triple confirmation with ATR trailing stops
  • MA CROSS — Moving Average crossover system with configurable periods
  • RSI REV — RSI mean-reversion strategy with oversold bounce entries

Each strategy generates a full equity curve, detailed trade log, and key performance metrics including total return, win rate, max drawdown, Sharpe ratio, and ATR stop-loss frequency.

🧠 Our Philosophy

We are not a trading signal service. We do not tell you what to buy or sell. Instead, we provide the framework for you to develop, test, and refine your own strategies.

  • Education over signals — Understanding why a strategy works matters more than following one blindly
  • Transparency — All strategy logic runs in your browser. Nothing is hidden behind a paywall
  • Risk first — We emphasize drawdown management and position sizing because survival matters more than any single trade
  • No financial advice — This is a simulation tool. Past performance does not guarantee future results
